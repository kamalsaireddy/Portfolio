About

A small thesis.

I studied Computer Science and moved into HR on purpose. The tools changed; the systems thinking didn't.

The best hires aren't found. They're designed.

Today my work has two halves. I close senior and management hires, and I run the HR operations that make a new hire's first day, and every day after it, actually work. I report the numbers plainly and write processes down so anyone can run them.

Now

What I'm doing right now.

At Techdome, Hyderabad, since November 2025

Leading hiring

I lead a Talent Acquisition team of 3 recruiters, from hiring plans to daily execution. I personally focus on senior and management roles: Business Analysts, Project and Product Managers, Project and Product Owners, and SRE, alongside IT and non-IT hiring across 12+ functions.

Running HR Ops

I own onboarding and induction, employee profiles in Keka HRMS, documentation and the employee lifecycle. I also run the HR Helpdesk on Jira, IT asset allocation, and vendors for background verification and workspace expansion.

Writing policy

I drafted the Recruitment Incentive Policy and the Employee Engagement SOP, and enhanced the Hybrid Work Policy.

Building culture

I run Fun Fridays, festival celebrations, wellness activities and team-building for the Hyderabad office. This work earned me the Rockstar Rookie and Team Player awards.

Bringing in AI

I'm moving screening, interview scheduling and candidate communication onto AI-assisted workflows, so recruiters spend their time on conversations instead of admin.
